# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains the main categories of information that may be processed when you use Density.

## 1. Information you choose to provide

Depending on how you use Density, this may include account information, provider configuration, project information, and content you submit to the AI providers you enable.

## 2. Local project access

Density can access files and run commands on your Android device when you explicitly allow the app to do so. Files remain under your device's storage permissions and project access choices.

## 3. Third-party AI providers

If you configure an AI provider, the prompts, code, files, or other content you send to that provider may be processed by the provider under its own privacy policy and terms.

## 4. Service and technical data

The website or app may process technical information needed to operate, secure, and maintain the service. The exact information and retention depend on the specific feature and configuration in use.

## 5. Your choices

You can stop using a provider by removing or disabling its configuration in Density. You can also contact us about privacy questions or requests.

## 6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact [densityapp@proton.me](mailto:densityapp@proton.me).
